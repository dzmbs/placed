# Local male wardrobe

`male-athletic-body.glb`, `male-casual-outfit.glb`, and
`male-athletic-outfit.glb` are derived from MakeHuman CC0 assets using Blender
and MPFB 2.0.17. The derivative geometry/materials and thumbnails are shared
under CC0-1.0. MPFB itself is GPL software; its software license does not
change the license of these exported assets.

Sources:

- [MakeHuman license](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/about/license.html)
- [MakeHuman system assets](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html)
- [CC0 asset packs](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks.html)
- [MPFB 2.0.17 source](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/tree/v2.0.17)
- [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

Used system assets: the hm08 base human, `young_caucasian_male2` skin,
`low-poly` eyes with brown material, `eyebrow001`, `eyelashes01`, `short01`
hair, `male_casualsuit06` and `shoes05`. The shirt and jeans were separated;
the shorts were cut from the fitted jeans, with a fabric edge added. Brand
art was removed from the default shirt. Authoring source license notices
identify Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, and Jonas Hauquier.

The separate `female-ivory.glb` is an original Meshy-generated evaluation
asset and is not part of this CC0 source inventory.

## Local female wardrobe

`female-shared-body.glb`, `female-casual-outfit.glb`,
`female-athletic-outfit.glb` and `female-gown-outfit.glb`, plus their
thumbnails, are derived from CC0 assets and shared under CC0-1.0.

System assets: hm08 female body, `young_caucasian_female2` skin,
`low-poly` brown eyes, `eyebrow001`, `eyelashes01`, `ponytail01`,
`female_casualsuit01`, `female_sportsuit01`, and `shoes05`.
The default T-shirt branding was removed, clothing materials replaced,
and the sneakers' tall socks trimmed to prevent trouser intersections.

The long gown derives from Margaret Toigo's
`toigo_halter_dress_with_fluted_skirt` / `Dress_Long_Fluted_Skirt`,
explicitly marked CC0 in its source and the official
[dress01 pack](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/dress01.html).
The fluted train was narrowed and its material replaced with plain ivory.
The pack was downloaded from the linked official MakeHuman files mirror.
The existing Meshy `female-ivory.glb` remains a distinct evaluation asset.
